I am a Senior Software Engineer, Tech Lead, and Researcher at NVIDIA. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at the Robotics and AI Institute (formerly the Boston Dynamics AI Institute). My research focuses on enabling robots to learn from human demonstration videos and interact with the physical world with dexterity, intelligence, and robustness.

I earned my Ph.D. from the University of California, Berkeley, where I was advised by Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka, a distinguished member of the National Academy of Engineering.
